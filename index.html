The residence

A two-story residence of 4,630 square feet behind a full-width gallery porch.

Five bedrooms and five and a half baths, with the primary suite on the main floor, a study, a game room and a loft. A two-story foyer opens to the family room, kitchen and dining room, and the family room opens to a covered outdoor living area. Upstairs, three rooms step out onto a covered balcony that runs the width of the house.
